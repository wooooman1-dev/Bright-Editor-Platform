# Product Principles

## Core Principles

-   Content Quality Above Everything Else
-   Users only focus on content.
-   AI is a quiet partner.
-   Continue Working by default.
-   Never Make Users Think.
-   Every Click Must Have a Purpose.
-   Simplicity First.
-   Quality Before Publish.

## Mission

Help creators publish content they are proud of.

## Success Metrics

-   Excellent content quality
-   Fast content creation
-   Seamless resume experience
-   Minimal learning curve
