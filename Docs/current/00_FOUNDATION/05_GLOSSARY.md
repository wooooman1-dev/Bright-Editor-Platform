# Bright Editor Platform

## Additional Glossary
Workspace: A user's independent working space. It owns Projects and may contain optional Brands.
Brand: An optional project classification and operating context inside one Workspace.
Project: A goal that always belongs to one Workspace and may optionally be associated with one Brand.
Content: A deliverable that always belongs to one Project, never directly to a Workspace or Brand.
Content Domain: Subject area such as Health, Finance, IT.
Knowledge Layer: Domain-specific knowledge abstraction.
Canonical Content: Master content reused across platforms.
Platform Adapter: Converts canonical content for each platform.
Content Transformation: Creates optimized platform-specific variants.
